# UPCOMING MATCH PLAYBOOK
## Live Match Operations "Cheat-Sheet" for the Admin

> **Document Type:** Operational Concept & Admin Guide  
> **System:** Sportsfan360 Second-Screen Live Match Platform  

---

### Core Concept: What is the Upcoming Match Playbook?

> **Think of the Upcoming Match Playbook simply as a "Live Match Cheat-Sheet" for the Admin.**

---

### Why do we need this Playbook?

* In the **finished match** (NZ vs IND 1st T20I), we already knew all the scores and wickets beforehand because the match was over.
* But in an **upcoming live match**, **nobody knows what will happen next**:
  * A batter might suddenly take a **DRS review**.
  * Two wickets might fall in one over (**a collapse**).
  * A team might hit 25 runs in an over (**a boundary burst**).
  * It might **rain**.

During a live cricket match, balls are bowled every 40 seconds. **The admin does not have time to sit and invent new questions from scratch.**

> **The Upcoming Playbook solves this by giving the admin ready-made questions and clear action rules so they can push engagements in 2 seconds!**

---

## The Playbook has 3 Simple Parts:

### Part 1: "If THIS Happens $\rightarrow$ Do THAT" (The Decision Rules)

Whenever a sudden key moment happens on TV, the admin simply checks this sheet:

#### 1. If an Umpire / DRS Review happens:
* **Rule**: Don't let people guess after seeing the slow-motion TV replay!
* **Admin Action**: Instantly freeze predictions and push:  
  👉 **"DRS CHECK! Was that OUT or NOT OUT?"** *(Options: Out / Not Out / Umpire's Call)*
* **Resolution**: Wait until the 3rd umpire's decision flashes on the big screen, then reveal the answer.

#### 2. If 2 Wickets fall quickly (A Mini-Collapse):
* **Rule**: Don't spam fans with 3 different cards.
* **Admin Action**: Cancel routine cards and push 1 big debate card:  
  👉 **"Collapse Alert! Two wickets down! Rebuild safely with singles or keep attacking?"**

#### 3. If a Team reaches 100 runs (Milestone):
* **Admin Action**: Push a score-band prediction:  
  👉 **"100 up! What will be the final score?"** *(<180 / 180–199 / 200–219 / 220+)*
* **Notice**: This question takes 8–10 overs to finish, so the post will move up. When it finally settles at the end of the innings, the **Floating Notification ("Jump to Post")** will bring users back to it!

---

### Part 2: The Ready-Made Questions (Fill-in-the-Blank Templates)

The admin has a list of pre-written questions with **4-minute timers**. They just fill in the team or player name and click **PUSH**:

1. **Before the match:**  
   `"{{team}} won the toss and elected to bat. Was this the right call?"`
2. **During Powerplay (Over 3):**  
   `"Where will {{team}} finish at the end of 6 overs?"` *(Under 45 / 45–59 / 60–74 / 75+)*
3. **When a star batter gets out:**  
   `"{{batter}} is out for {{runs}}! Does the bowling team have the upper hand now?"`
4. **At the Innings Break:**  
   `"{{team}} need {{target}} runs to win. Who wins the match?"`
5. **In the Final Over (Over 19):**  
   `"Final over thriller! {{runs}} needed from 6 balls. Who takes the victory?"`

---

### Part 3: The 3 Core Rules the Playbook Reminds the Admin

#### 1. Every card has a 4-Minute Timer:
* Every question the admin pushes gives fans exactly **4 minutes** (ticking from `04:00` down to `00:00`) to vote, predict, and comment before it auto-locks.

#### 2. If the post moves up $\rightarrow$ Floating Notification brings them back:
* If a prediction resolves 2 or 3 overs later, the admin reveals the answer, and a floating button appears inside the room so users can tap and jump straight back to that post.

#### 3. Every 6 Overs $\rightarrow$ Recognitions for EVERY fan:
* At Over 6, Over 12, Over 18, and Innings End, the admin triggers the **7 Predefined Recognitions** so that *every single participating fan* (even someone who only voted in 3 out of 5 posts or 1 post) receives a badge and positive recognition!

---

## Summary in One Sentence

> **The Upcoming Playbook is simply a pre-written guide and question bank so that during your upcoming test match, the admin knows exactly what to push in 2 seconds whenever a DRS, wicket, or milestone happens on TV, with the 4-minute timer, floating jump notifications, and 6-over recognitions running smoothly!**
